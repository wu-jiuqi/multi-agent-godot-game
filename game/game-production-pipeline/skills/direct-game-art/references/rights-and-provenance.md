# Rights and Provenance

## Principle

Visual suitability and legal usability are separate gates. Maintain a provenance register from research through final source and runtime packages. When the project lacks qualified legal review, report the factual record and risk; never claim legal clearance on inference alone.

## Reference record

For every external reference record creator/title, stable URL or source, access date, intended use, rights category, attribution requirement, applicable license/terms snapshot, and anti-copy note. Use these categories consistently:

- `owned`: created for and controlled by the project;
- `licensed`: used under recorded terms;
- `public-domain`: status supported by jurisdiction-appropriate evidence;
- `reference-only`: inspiration/research only, not distributable production input;
- `unknown`: blocked from production.

Use SPDX license expressions when a standard software/content license applies, and preserve the actual terms snapshot for marketplace assets, fonts, textures, scans, model releases, or custom commissions.

## Generative AI record

The Art Direction Contract declares `forbidden`, `research-only`, `concept-only`, or `allowed`. For every allowed use, record provider, model, version/date, terms snapshot, prompt/input asset provenance, consent/restriction status, output disposition, and a concrete reference to human-authored selection, editing, paint-over, modeling, composition, or other contribution. Never use an AI-generated image as proof of cleared training data or exclusive rights.

Treat model changes, terms changes, and undisclosed input assets as provenance changes that invalidate the applicable rights review. Protect confidential project assets and personal data according to project policy.

## Release checklist

- final source and runtime files map to provenance entries;
- licenses permit the intended commercial use, modification, platform distribution, and raw/source redistribution where applicable;
- attribution, notice, model/property releases, and geographic restrictions are captured;
- commissioned work has recorded ownership/license terms;
- fonts, logos, trademarks, cultural material, and recognizable persons receive explicit review;
- AI records and human contribution evidence are complete when applicable;
- a designated rights reviewer signs the current contract digest.

## Reference basis

- SPDX license-expression syntax: https://spdx.github.io/spdx-spec/v3.0.1/annexes/spdx-license-expressions/
- SPDX 3.0 scope includes AI models, datasets, provenance, and licensing metadata: https://spdx.github.io/spdx-spec/v3.0.1/scope/
- U.S. Copyright Office AI initiative and copyrightability report explain the continuing importance of human authorship in that jurisdiction: https://www.copyright.gov/ai/ and https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf
- WIPO overview of copyright and video games: https://www.wipo.int/export/sites/www/cooperation/en/docs/ip-video-games.pdf

Legal rules differ by jurisdiction and change over time; obtain qualified review for release-critical conclusions.
