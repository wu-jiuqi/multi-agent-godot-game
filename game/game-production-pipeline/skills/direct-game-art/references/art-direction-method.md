# Art Direction Method

## Operating model

Art direction is a decision system, not a collection of attractive references. Start from the intended player experience and gameplay information, turn them into visual rules, prove those rules in representative content, then use the frozen rules to guide production and review.

Use five linked layers:

1. **Player effect:** what the player should feel, notice, predict, and remember.
2. **Design constraints:** camera, verbs, pacing, audience, platforms, engine, team, time, and budget.
3. **Direction options:** distinct visual hypotheses with explicit signatures and trade-offs.
4. **Visual language:** reusable rules for shape, value, color, surface, light, composition, graphics, motion, effects, and UI.
5. **Production proof:** source-ready specifications plus an in-engine benchmark under actual performance and readability conditions.

## Research protocol

- Begin major art work with fresh web research. Prefer first-party talks, official documentation, museum/archive material, and primary cultural sources.
- Record every source as evidence, not as an instruction to imitate. Capture the principle learned, its intended application, and a specific anti-copy note.
- Include game references for production precedent and non-game references for originality: architecture, industrial design, fashion, illustration, photography, typography, film, craft, nature, or a relevant historical source.
- Separate `owned/licensed/public-domain` material from `reference-only` material. Reference-only material cannot enter final source packages.
- Use video when motion, transitions, camera, VFX timing, or production practice is the subject. Timestamp the relevant passage in project evidence when practical.

## Option quality bar

Create at least three options whose differences survive grayscale, silhouette, and UI translation. Each option needs:

- a one-sentence thesis and memorable signature;
- the player effect and design-pillar fit;
- shape, value/color, material/light, graphic, motion, and VFX tendencies;
- how it appears in characters/environment/props/UI as applicable;
- implementation cost, content throughput, technical risk, accessibility risk, and likely failure mode;
- a small evidence set and a reason it is not merely a derivative copy.

Recommend one option, but keep the final direction choice with the designated human. A rejected option remains useful evidence and must not be silently recycled into the selected direction.

## Feedback protocol

Feedback must identify the observation, violated rule or target player effect, priority, responsible owner, required change, and recheck. Avoid taste-only notes such as “make it pop.” When the rule itself is wrong, revise the Contract and invalidate downstream approvals; do not disguise a direction change as an asset correction.

## Research basis

- GDC, *Building a Unique Art Direction Without Breaking the Game*: art pillars, constraints, visual language, references, anti-references, readability, and team capacity. https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1028954/Art-Direction-Summit-Building-a
- Riot Games Art Education, *Intro to Game Art* and *Concept Art*: game art serves gameplay; concept work is iterative problem-solving under constraints. https://www.riotgames.com/en/artedu/intro-to-game-art and https://www.riotgames.com/id/artedu/concept-art
- Riot Games R&D, *Collaborative Creative in R&D*: visual exploration, cross-discipline collaboration, and prototype art belong in early development. https://www.riotgames.com/en/r-and-d-office/collaborative-creative-in-r-d
- GDC, *Pre-Production Art Direction*: use the art bible and deliberate evaluation points to make exploration predictable before production. https://gdcvault.com/play/1034593/Art-Direction-Summit-Pre-Production

These sources support the method; they do not grant rights to reproduce their images or slides.
